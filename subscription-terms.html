The following are the terms for FilmArchive Pro auto-renewable subscriptions (pro.monthly / pro.yearly). The Pro lifetime purchase (pro.lifetime) is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription. The Pro lifetime option is a one-time purchase and does not renew.
